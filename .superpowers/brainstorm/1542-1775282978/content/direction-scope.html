Direction filter — global or per-station?

The direction filter hides departures going the wrong way (e.g. away from Stockholm). Should it apply to everything, or be set per station?

A

Global — one setting for all stations

One dropdown at the top of the configurator: Towards Stockholm / All directions / Custom keywords. Applies to every station section on the dashboard.

Matches how the URL works today: ?direction=stockholm

B

Per-station — each entry has its own direction

Each station card in the list gets its own direction setting. Useful if you commute from two different areas with different "towards" concepts.
